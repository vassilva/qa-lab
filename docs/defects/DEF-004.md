## Summary
Between two buy orders at the same price, the one that arrived **later** executes first. On the sell side the rule is correct.

## Steps to reproduce
1. QA environment in challenge mode, clean market.
2. Buy 100 EDP at 4.48 (O00001). 3. Buy 100 EDP at 4.48 (O00002).
4. Sell 100 EDP at 4.48.

## Expected result
The trade is with **O00001** (it arrived first).

## Actual result
`{"buy_order_id":"O00002","sell_order_id":"O00003"}`. The same test with two sell orders correctly executes the first one (O00001).

## Evidence
- Log: `INFO [ME] Trade T00001: 100 EDP @ 4.48 (buy O00002 / sell O00003)`
- The comparison test on the sell side passes, which isolates the problem in the sorting of the bid side of the book.

## Analysis
The bid side of the book is sorted by arrival time in reverse. This breaks the price-time rule, which is the basis of a fair market.
No price impact, but a regulatory and reputational impact.
