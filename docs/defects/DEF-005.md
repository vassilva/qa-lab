# DEF-005 · Settlement calendar ignores TARGET2 holidays

## Summary
TARGET2 closing days (Christmas, 26 December, Good Friday, Easter Monday, 1 January, 1 May) are treated
as business days: the settlement date falls on a holiday and a holiday can be set as the business date.

## Steps to reproduce
1. QA environment in challenge mode.
2. Set the business date to 2026-12-23 and execute a trade of 100 EDP at 4.50.
3. Look at the settlement instruction.
4. Try to set the business date to 2026-12-25.

## Expected result
Intended settlement on **2026-12-28** (25 and 26 are holidays, 27 is a Sunday). The date 2026-12-25 is refused (HTTP 400).

## Actual result
- `{"instruction_id":"S00001","trade_date":"2026-12-23","intended_settlement_date":"2026-12-25"}`
- `POST /api/business-date {"date":"2026-12-25"}` → accepted (`{"business_date":"2026-12-25"}`).

## Evidence
- Log: `INFO [SETTLEMENT] Instruction S00001 created ... intended settlement 2026-12-25`
- BDD: the holiday examples (Christmas and Easter) fail; the weekend examples pass, which isolates the problem in the holidays.

## Analysis
The business-day calculation only excludes weekends. Instructions dated on a day when T2S is closed do not settle and cause
settlement fails and penalties. Blocks the release.
