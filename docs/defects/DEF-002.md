# DEF-002 · Trade executed at the incoming order's price instead of the book price

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Status | Open (challenge mode) |
| Component | Matching Engine |
| Severity | Critical |
| Priority | P1 |
| Environment | QA · challenge mode (`QA_LAB_CHALLENGE=1`) |
| Version | 0.3.0-c200193 |
| Acceptance criterion | AC-N01 |
| Risk | R-01 |
| Detected by | BDD scenario `A crossing buy executes at the price of the resting sell` |
| Date | 2026-11-03 |

## Summary
When a buy crosses a sell that was already resting in the book, the trade is done at the buy price (the incoming order)
instead of the sell price (the resting order). The buyer pays more than they should.

## Steps to reproduce
1. QA environment in challenge mode, clean market.
2. Sell 100 EDP at 4.50.
3. Buy 100 EDP at 4.55.

## Expected result
1 trade of 100 at **4.50** (the price of the resting order, AC-N01).

## Actual result
`{"trade_id":"T00001","qty":100,"price":4.55,"buy_order_id":"O00002","sell_order_id":"O00001"}`

## Evidence
- Log: `INFO [ME] Trade T00001: 100 EDP @ 4.55 (buy O00002 / sell O00001)`
- SQL: `SELECT trade_id, price FROM trades;` → `T00001 | 4.55`
- The same defect makes the "Best price has priority over arrival time" scenario fail (trade at 4.55 instead of 4.51).

## Analysis
The Matching Engine uses the aggressor order's price. Direct financial impact on every trade that crosses with price improvement
(0.05 EUR × 100 = 5 EUR in this example) and a breach of market rules. Blocks the release.
