# DEF-003 · A partial fill does not reduce the sell order in the book: it sells more than it had

## Summary
When a sell order is partially filled, its remaining quantity does not go down. The order stays in the book with its original quantity
and can be executed again: a sell order of 100 ended up executing 140.

## Steps to reproduce
1. QA environment in challenge mode, clean market.
2. Sell 100 EDP at 4.50 (O00001).
3. Buy 40 EDP at 4.50.
4. Buy 100 EDP at 4.50.

## Expected result
After step 3, the sell order is `PARTIALLY_FILLED` with **60** remaining. Step 4 only executes 60.

## Actual result
- After step 3: `{"order_id":"O00001","qty":100,"remaining":100,"status":"PARTIALLY_FILLED"}`; the book shows 100.
- Step 4 executes **100**: the sell order of 100 executed 40 + 100 = **140**.

## Evidence
- SQL (preset "[Trading] Executed qty vs. order"): `O00001 | qty 100 | remaining 0 | executed 140`
- Log: two trades against sell order O00001, of 40 and of 100.

## Analysis
The engine does not reduce the remaining quantity of the resting order on a partial fill (only on the sell side).
Consequence: a short sale, i.e. delivering securities the participant does not hold; settlement would fail. The most serious defect on the list.
The SQL integrity check detects it without looking at the book, which shows the value of database checks.
